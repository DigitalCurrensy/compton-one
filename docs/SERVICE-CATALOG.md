## What the expanded sourcing exercise showed

- **13 of 22 routes have no dedicated online intake form.** They are phone-first. Every one of them is a barrier for a resident who works business hours, or who is Deaf or hard of hearing.
- **Streetlights still fork by pole ownership**, and now sit next to a separate power-outage route — a distinction residents do not make and should not have to.
- The City's own "I Want To…" menu already tries to solve this problem: *"find City services without having to know which department offers the service."* The app is the same idea, carried through to a receipt and a follow-up date.

## Deliberate omissions

Individual staff desk lines appear in the public directory and are **not** surfaced. They are not intake lines, and routing residents to a named employee's desk is both a privacy problem and operationally wrong.

Still out of scope, and refused honestly: voter registration · passports · park facility rentals · towed vehicles · CalFresh and county benefits · school enrolment · jury duty · library services · job programmes. Each is a real service; none is in the catalog, so the app says so and gives the verified main line.

## No SLA claims

No published response time was found for any of the 22. `officialSlaConfirmed` is `false` across the board and a test enforces it.
